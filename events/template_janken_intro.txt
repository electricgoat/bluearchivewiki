{{name}} is a rock-paper-scissors minigame. Each turn the player and the opponent pick a hand at the same time; winning the exchange deals damage based on attack power, while a draw deals reduced damage to both sides. The goal is to reduce the opponent's HP to zero within the turn limit.

The player fights with one of the crabs below, equipped with a single accessory; both contribute attack power and HP. Playing a hand charges skill cost, which is spent on the crab's active skill.

Entering a stage costs {{wiki_play_cost}}, while raising equipment tiers costs {{wiki_upgrade_cost}}.
