[[File:Virtual Aptitude Test.png|thumb|400px|Virtual Aptitude Test BETA]]
'''Virtual Aptitude Test''' (仮想適性試験; Tactical Relay internally) is a battle mode in which up to {{intro.squads}} squads take turns fighting waves of enemies. Its first run, '''Virtual Aptitude Test BETA''', is held in the Japanese version from {{intro.period}}. The mode opens after clearing [[Missions/{{intro.unlock}}|Mission {{intro.unlock}}]], and [[Plana]] runs the tests.

==Overview==
[[File:Virtual Aptitude Test formation.png|thumb|400px|Squads and a stage's mission info]]
There are {{stages|length}} stages, each with {{levels|length}} levels: {{levels[:-1]|map(attribute='level')|join(', ')}} and {{levels[-1].level}}. Only level {{levels[0].level}} is open at first, and clearing a level opens the next one. A stage's enemies come in sections of {{(levels[0].waves / levels[0].sections)|int}} waves, and their armor alternates between two types from one section to the next.

Battle rewards depend on the furthest wave reached. Once a stage has been cleared, sweep function is unlocked; note that sweep gives the rewards of the best record achieved for given stage.

The mission info screen ranks Sensei's records on each stage by level, waves cleared and clear time, and shows ''Tallying'' until {{intro.ranking}} Senseis have cleared the stage. The records don't show on the profile.

Being a beta, the details of mode mechanics are subject to change.

==Squads==
[[File:Virtual Aptitude Test TCS and affection rank buff.png|thumb|400px|TCS slots and the Affection Rank Buff]]
Aptitude Test squad has 4 Strikers, 2 Specials, and 2 TCS slots that accept any student class, for up to 8 students. One student per squad can be borrowed from another Sensei.

Students in TCS slots get no EX skill cards: they use their EX skills by themselves at set times instead, and Specials in TCS slots still lend the Strikers their stats.

==Battle==
[[File:Virtual Aptitude Test relay.png|thumb|400px|Switching squads during a battle]]
One squad fights at a time. Squads can be switched during the battle, so that each section's enemies meet a squad suited to their armor. In AUTO play the squads can switch, and Limit Break can be used automatically, both can be configured in game options.

===Extra HP===
[[File:Virtual Aptitude Test extra HP.png|thumb|400px|Enemies with extra HP]]
Enemies appear with extra HP equal to {{battle.extra_hp.amount}} of their max HP, which runs out over {{battle.extra_hp.duration}} seconds.
{{'{{'}}Clear{{'}}'}}

===Limit Break===
[[File:Virtual Aptitude Test Limit Break.png|thumb|400px|Skills costing nothing during Limit Break]]
Damaging enemies and meeting the stage's special rule charges the Limit Break gauge. Limit Break uses up a full gauge, and makes all student EX skills cost nothing for {{battle.limit_break.duration}} seconds.

The gauge capacity grows by {{battle.limit_break.step}} for every {{battle.limit_break.every}} affection ranks of all owned students together, up to +{{battle.limit_break.most}} at {{battle.limit_break.up_to}}.
{{'{{'}}Clear{{'}}'}}

===Other rules===
[[File:Virtual Aptitude Test RETURN.png|thumb|400px|RETURN]]
* RETURN brings a squad whose students have scattered across the field back together.
* Strikers can't be damaged for {{battle.immunity}} seconds after entering the field.
* EX skill animations are always off.
