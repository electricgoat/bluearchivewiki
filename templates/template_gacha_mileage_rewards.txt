===First-time rewards===
First-time rewards can each be claimed once per pickup recruitment period. After the final first-time reward, progress continues into the repeat rewards.

===Repeat rewards===
Repeat rewards follow the first-time rewards and cycle infinitely. After the final repeat reward, the sequence starts again from its first reward.

The number in parentheses is the cumulative recruitment count at which the reward is first available.
