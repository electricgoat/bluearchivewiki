The '''Battle Pass''' (バトルパス) is a reward track that runs in seasons of a few weeks. The Japanese version got it on {{launch.JP}}{% if launch.GL %}, the Global version on {{launch.GL}}{% endif %}.

The game presents it as a TV shopping program: [[Shinon|Kawaru Shinon]] of the Kronos School of Journalism hosts a special live broadcast selling the pass's paid track, '''Growth CH''' (成長CH), while a news ticker runs headlines from around Kivotos, a new set each season. Shinon's lines on the pass's screens are on [[Shinon/audio]].

==Overview==
Players earn pass points by completing the pass missions: daily and weekly missions, along with one-off seasonal achievements. Every {{intro.level_points}} points raise the pass level by one, up to level {{intro.max_level}}. The points of daily and weekly missions are capped each week, the weeks starting on {{intro.week_start}} (UTC+9). Levels can also be bought, for {{intro.level_price}} each.

Each level gives a reward on the free track, '''Basic CH''' (一般CH), and one on Growth CH to Sensei who bought one of the season's offers:
* '''Growth CH''' unlocks the Growth CH rewards.
* '''Growth CH+''' unlocks them too, raises the pass level by {{intro.bonus_levels}} at once, and comes with the season's title and Pyroxenes.
* Growth CH can be upgraded to the CH+ version by additionally paying the difference in their price.
